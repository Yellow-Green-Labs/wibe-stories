This is a temporary demo article with real markdown headings, so the On this page card has something to navigate.

## Record Your Voice

Open the app, press the mic, and speak. Your voice is captured as audio and sent for transcription.

### Choose Your Language

Pick the language you are speaking before you record, or let the app detect it automatically.

### Dictate with Wispr Flow

Prefer typing? Wispr Flow turns your speech into text that lands straight in the message box.

## Style the Card

The text becomes a card you can design before sharing.

### Pick an Occasion

Occasions come with themed backgrounds and translations, so the card speaks the right language.

### Adjust the Tone

Rewrite the message in a warmer, funnier, or more formal voice with one tap.

## Share It

Copy the link or send the card straight to family and friends. It opens on any phone, in their own language.

### It Expires, But You Keep the Memory

Cards are temporary by design, but you can save yours to the Wibe Vault before they vanish.
