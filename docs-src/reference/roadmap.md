# Roadmap

*Last updated: Jul 17, 2026*

This roadmap outlines what we're building next and what we've recently shipped. Priorities and timelines are subject to change.

### Planned

- **Vault improvements** — Download and share cards directly from your vault, with sort, search, and better organization tools.
- **Pro Calendar Drawer** — Browse upcoming occasions on a calendar and create occasion cards with one tap (Pro only).
- **Key Status modal** — View your Pro key tier, expiry date, and days remaining at a glance (Pro only).

### Considering

- **Batch create** — Write or record once and generate cards in all 7 tone styles at once.

### Recently Shipped

- **Wibe Pass pricing** — Stackable credit system replacing subscriptions. Four tiers from $6 to $60.
- **Pro vault** — Server-side card storage with 50-card limit, cross-device sync via Pro key.
- **Occasion email campaigns** — Daily occasion reminders for 30 global dates.
- **Custom color picker** — Full color wheel with sliders for Pro users.
- **46 speech languages, 11 UI locales** — Expanded language support.
- **7 tone styles** — Preview and commit rewrite flow with per-tone daily limits.
